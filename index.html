Mileage update

Regular maintenance update

Upload the big parts file (Car, Mileage, Del Date, Item/Service Description). For every vehicle in the chosen warehouse, the mileage of the MOST RECENT replacement (by Del Date) of the selected item is prepared (when the highest mileage is not the most recent entry, you choose which one to use) for that item's column in the warehouse sheet. You review the list, untick any vehicle you don't want, then press Update selected. Vehicles whose new mileage is lower than the current one are listed separately and are not ticked.
Print all the maintenance needed in the selected warehouse.
